Our Philosophy

At Pudology, we believe food should be nutritious, delicious, and convenient. Our avocado-based protein puddings combine protein, fiber, and nutrient-dense ingredients.

We focus on creating satisfying treats made with thoughtfully selected ingredients, including superfoods and natural sweeteners.

What Makes Pudology Different?

  • Avocado-based pudding recipes
  • Protein and fiber in every serving
  • Mostly organic ingredients
  • Thoughtfully selected superfoods
  • No granulated sugar added to our recipes
