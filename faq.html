Frequently Asked Questions

About Our Puddings

What are Pudology puddings made from?

Our puddings are made with avocados, protein, and carefully selected ingredients, including superfoods.

Are your puddings gluten-free?

Our pudding recipes do not use gluten-containing ingredients. Please contact us about specific allergen concerns.

How are your puddings sweetened?

Depending on the flavor, we use sweeteners such as monk fruit or honey. Check each product's ingredients for more information.

Do your puddings contain dairy or nuts?

Many of our puddings contain milk and whey protein. Some flavors also contain peanuts or tree nuts. Please ask about allergens before ordering.

Storage and Ordering

How should I store my puddings?

Keep puddings refrigerated. Follow the storage instructions provided with your order.

Where can I buy Pudology?

You can find us at farmers markets throughout San Diego County. Visit our Find Us page for locations.

How can I contact Pudology?

Visit our Contact Us page or connect with us on Instagram.
